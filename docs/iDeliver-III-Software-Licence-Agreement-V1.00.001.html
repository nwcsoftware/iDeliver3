Acceptance of these terms by a Second Party constitutes the Subscription Agreement between that Second Party and the Licensee, and is a condition of access to the Module.

The free month is granted once per Second Party and is not repeated on renewal, reactivation, or on registration of a further account for the same underlying business.
  2. Standard monthly subscription. After the free introductory month, access continues under the Standard Subscription at USD 10.00 per month, per supplier or per partner, each Second Party being counted separately.
  3. Prices subject to change. The rate stated above is the rate in force at the date of this Appendix and is subject to change, in particular where the costs charged by hosting, database, mapping, messaging, authentication, payment or other third-party providers change, or where taxes or regulatory charges are imposed. Any revised rate applies from the next monthly period following notice to the Second Party, who may decline it by not renewing.
  4. Payment & activation. Subscriptions are payable monthly in advance. A Second Party account created in the Software remains inactive and unable to sign in until payment is confirmed and the subscription is activated by the Licensee’s administrator. Access ends automatically when the subscribed period expires and resumes upon confirmation of the following period.

  6. Compliance with the delivery provider’s rules. Suppliers and partners shall at all times comply with the rules, policies, operating procedures, pricing conditions and service standards of the delivery provider as published or notified from time to time, including those governing product listings, prices, photographs, commissions, packaging, order preparation times, returns and customer communication. The administrator of the Software may, at any time and without prior notice, deactivate or suspend a supplier or partner from using the Module — including for non-payment, breach of the rules referred to above, suspected fraud or misuse, legal or regulatory reasons, or protection of customers or of the platform.

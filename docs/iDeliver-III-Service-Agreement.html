3. The Delivered System — Overview & Architecture

iDeliver III is an integrated, multi-user platform that manages the full lifecycle of a delivery business: from taking an order, through dispatch and driver assignment, delivery, cash collection, and final financial reconciliation. It is delivered as four connected applications sharing a single secure database:

Module A Operations Console & Call Center — the desktop back-office application used by staff.
Module B Partners & Suppliers Portal — a restricted view for external partners/suppliers.
Module C Driver Application — the field app drivers use on delivery.
Module D Customer Mobile Application — the app end-customers use to book & track.

Technical foundation

  • Desktop application packaged for Windows (and buildable for macOS / Linux), installable on office workstations.
  • Secure cloud database & backend with role-based access control and row-level security, so every user sees only the data their role permits.
  • Real-time shared data — orders, drivers, payments and messages are synchronised across all four modules.
  • Roles supported: Super-Administrator (Developer), Administrator, Office/Call-Center user, Partner, Supplier, Driver, and Customer.
  • Multi-currency handling of order totals and collections; interactive maps for locations & tracking; built-in PDF/report generation.
  • Security: individual user accounts, enforced password change on first login / after reset, and administrator-managed account provisioning.

4. Module A — Operations Console & Call Center (Desktop)

The desktop application is the operational heart of the business — the Call Center and back-office console. It includes the following delivered capabilities:

Dispatch & Order Management (Call Center)

Live Dashboard with real-time stats: available drivers, orders in transit, pending, failed / cancelled.
Order intake & management — create and edit delivery orders with customer, addresses and instructions.
Order composition: goods/items, services, packages, and returnable items on a single order.
Driver assignment and status progression (pending → in transit → delivered / failed / cancelled).
Closed Orders screen with Outlook-style date grouping for historical review.
Location tagging and pickup / drop-off addresses with map selection.
Customer quick-search / combobox for fast order entry at the call center.
Free-order and credit-order handling (orders that close without immediate payment).

Live Tracking & Drivers

Map-based tracking of orders and delivery locations.
Drivers register — manage drivers, availability and assignment.
Vehicles register linked to the fleet.
Driver availability reflected live on the dashboard.

Cash, Payments & Financial Reconciliation

Money lifecycle: driver collects cash → Driver Settlements → Cashier Box, fully tracked end-to-end.
Driver Settlements — reconcile cash owed by each driver and mark it paid to the office.
Cashier Box — the office cash ledger with a reversible reset checkpoint.
Credit Customers — account-level dues, collection and printable statements.
Partner Dues — per-partner package balances and settlement.
Multi-currency totals and collections throughout.

Catalog, Purchasing & Retail

Products catalog with automatic serial coding by kind (PRD / SRV / RTN / ADD) and service flagging.
Price List management.
Returnable Items tracking.
Purchase Invoices (procurement).
Retail Invoices (point-of-sale style sales).
Supplier Settlements — procurement commission & month-end supplier reports.

Contacts & Accounting

Contacts managed as Customers, Suppliers and Partners.
Multiple addresses per contact with interactive map picker.
Chart-of-Accounts sub-account numbers per contact.
Account Statements and Account Transactions ledgers.
Company profile and Reports with charts & analytics.
On-screen and PDF-exportable statements & reports.

Communication & Administration

Broadcast Messages — send announcements to users, with per-user unread indicators & pop-ups.
User Accounts administration — create users, assign roles, reset passwords.
Enforced first-login / post-reset password change.
App Settings and company configuration.

Super-Administrator (Developer) Tools

Developer Account management (restricted).
Delete Order and Delete Orders by Date (bulk, selective, super-admin only).
Reset Cashier Box — reversible checkpoint that hides movements as of a date without deleting.
Reset Data and controlled maintenance operations.

5. Module B — Partners & Suppliers Portal

A dedicated, restricted experience for external Partners and Suppliers. When a partner or supplier signs in, they are placed in their own portal and never see the full back-office application.

Partner / Supplier Portal capabilities

Access scoped to their own contact only — they see nothing that belongs to other parties.
Visibility limited to their Sold Orders and Completed Orders.
Supplier commission tracking and month-end settlement reporting.
Partner package dues — balances of packages handled on their behalf.
Separate, secure login governed by the same role & security model.
No access to dispatch, cashier, catalog, admin or other customers' data.

6. Module C — Driver Application

An external application for delivery drivers in the field, connected to the same live system. It is the first hop of the cash lifecycle and keeps delivery status current for the whole operation.

Driver Application capabilities

Drivers see the orders assigned to them for delivery.
Update delivery status (e.g. delivered / failed) from the field, reflected instantly in the office.
Record cash collected from the customer on delivery, attributed to the driver.
Collections flow automatically into Driver Settlements for office reconciliation.
Access limited strictly to the driver's own assignments and collections.
Keeps the office Tracking and Dashboard up to date in real time.

7. Module D — Customer Mobile Application

A customer-facing mobile application allowing end-customers to register, book deliveries, and track their orders.

Customer Mobile capabilities

Multi-language interface: English, Arabic (with right-to-left layout), French and Romanian.
Registration and secure sign-in, including one-time-passcode (OTP) and Google sign-in.
Home screen with quick actions and recent activity.
Book Delivery — create a delivery/collection request with one or more request lines.
Saved addresses with map-based location selection; delivery / drop and pickup points.
Payment preference — cash on delivery or credit/debit, where allowed.
My Orders — list, view details, and edit pending requests.
Live delivery status per order (awaiting pickup, in progress, delivered).
Notifications & broadcast messages from the operator.
Profile management (details, addresses, language, password).
Integration. Customer-created requests arrive directly in the Operations Console as external requests, ready for the call center to dispatch — closing the loop across all four modules.

9. Change Control & Additional Work

Purpose of this section. To keep the project stable and fairly priced for both parties, all new ideas, additions and modifications after Acceptance are handled through a single, written Change Control process. Verbal or ad-hoc requests do not create an obligation on the Developer and are not part of the delivered scope.
  1. Everything new is a Change Request. Any request that adds to, expands, or alters the Scope Baseline — including new screens, fields, reports, rules, integrations, third-party services, redesigns, or additional modules — is an Enhancement and is handled as a Change Request (CR), separate from this Agreement.
  2. How a Change Request is raised. The Client submits the request in writing (email is sufficient). The Developer responds with a written impact assessment: description, effort/time estimate, price, and any effect on other features or timelines.
  3. Approval before work. The Developer begins work on a CR only after the Client approves the written estimate and price. No approved estimate, no work — and no expectation of delivery.
  4. Pricing of changes. Change Requests are billed either at an hourly rate of USD 25.00 per hour or, where the scope allows, at a fixed price per CR quoted in advance, and are not included in any support or maintenance fee unless expressly agreed in writing.
  5. Bug fixes are not Change Requests. A genuine Defect — a Scope Baseline feature failing to work as described in this Agreement — is corrected under Section 10 and is not charged as a CR.
  6. Distinguishing defects from changes. A request to make a feature behave differently than described here (rather than fixing a failure to meet this description) is a Change Request, not a Defect.
  7. Batching & scheduling. To keep the System stable, the Developer may schedule approved changes into planned releases rather than delivering each request immediately.

10. Support, Maintenance & Hosting

  1. Warranty & Defect support. The Developer will correct verified Defects in the Scope Baseline for a warranty period of ninety (90) days from the Delivery Date (01 June 2026), i.e. until 30 August 2026, at no additional charge.
  2. Maintenance (optional). After the warranty period, ongoing maintenance — minor adjustments, updates, database upkeep and priority support — may be provided under a separate annual maintenance arrangement at USD 600.00 per year (payable annually in advance, equivalent to USD 50.00 per month). Maintenance does not include Enhancements (Section 9), which are quoted separately.
  3. Hosting & third-party services. The System relies on cloud database, mapping and authentication services. Their subscription / usage fees are the Client's responsibility, currently estimated at USD 25.00 – USD 120.00 per month depending on usage volume; the Developer will configure and maintain these services on the Client's behalf.
  4. Backups & data. During the warranty period and any active maintenance arrangement, the Developer performs automated daily database backups, retained for thirty (30) days. The Client remains responsible for exporting and retaining its own operational records (statements, reports and invoices) for its long-term needs.
  5. Response targets (developer's mandatory service levels). For issues reported in writing (email is sufficient), the Developer will use reasonable endeavours to meet the following times, counted in Beirut business days:
    1. Critical (System unusable / operations stopped): acknowledge within one (1) business day and provide a fix or workaround within three (3) business days.
    2. Major (a key feature failing, workaround exists): acknowledge within two (2) business days and resolve within seven (7) business days.
    3. Minor (cosmetic or low-impact): addressed in the next scheduled maintenance release.
  6. Support hours. Standard support is provided Monday to Friday, 09:00–17:00 (Beirut time), excluding official Lebanese public holidays.
